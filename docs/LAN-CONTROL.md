# Enable LAN Control

Lumen talks to Yeelight lights on your local network. It does not use a Yeelight account, and a light ignores it until LAN Control is turned on.

1. Open the Yeelight app on your phone and make sure the light is on the same Wi-Fi network as this computer.
2. Open the light, then its settings.
3. Turn on **LAN Control**.
4. In Lumen, choose **Search Again**.

A light that still does not appear can be added from the main menu with **Add Light by Address…**. Use the address shown by your router. The computer and the light must both be on a private network.

If a light used to respond and then stops accepting commands after running for a long time, turn it off and on at the wall switch. That clears a known firmware quota bug.
